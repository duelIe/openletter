To my dearest wife,

Thank you for coming into my life.
My life that was once plain and
bland, is now filled with vibrant
colors because of you. I know there
have been moments when I’ve done
things that did not sit well with you,
words that I’ve spoken that may
caused you pain. I deeply regret
them. I may not be perfect, but I am
willing to grow and become the best
version of myself--for you, for us.

Thank you for believing in me, for
strengthening my confidence, and
for lifting me up when I’m feeling
down. I will always cherish what we
share, and I am looking forward to
creating even more beautiful mem-
ories with you.
